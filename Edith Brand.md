# Edith — brand identity

## The idea
Edith is the editor you'd want in the room: calm, exact, unbothered by a messy draft. The name has *edit* baked in. You write; Edith keeps it clean and sends it on its way.

## Positioning
**Your Markdown files, finally readable.**

The problem Edith solves: Markdown is what AI tools, developers and wikis hand you, and it is miserable to read and fiddly to revise in a plain text editor. Edith opens those files as proper documents, lets you fix them without touching a single `#` or `**`, keeps every version so an experiment can be undone, and hands the result back as clean Markdown for Claude, Google Docs or a colleague.

One-liners for different audiences:
- Ops: *Edit the manual, not the markup.*
- Creative: *Draft, rework, and never lose the version you liked.*
- AI power users: *Paste from Claude, revise like a document, copy it back.*

## How Edith should make people feel
- *Relief* — "finally, I can just read this."
- *Composure* — nothing flashes, nothing nags, nothing is lost. Versions are kept; notes stay put.
- *Lightness* — the work of revising feels like folding paper, not operating machinery.

## The mark
An **origami paper boat** on a soft green tile, riding two light-green waves. A draft, folded neatly and set on its way — to Claude, to Docs, to a colleague. It is drawn as the classic folded silhouette (upturned hull, central peak) with light and shadow facets so the shape survives at 16 px. Two faint "text" marks on the peak, visible only at large sizes, say it was made from a page.

Rules: the boat never gets a flag, a sun, a face, or a horizon. The tile is always the green gradient; never put the boat on white. No wordmark inside the icon.

## The wordmark
`edith`, lowercase, set in IBM Plex Sans Semibold, warm ink. In prose, Edith takes a capital E.

| Parchment | `#F1E9D8` | Toolbar, sidebar, tab strip, chrome |
| Cream | `#FAF7EF` | The page itself |

Dusk (dark): surface `#3A352E`, chrome `#332E28`, text `#ECE4D2`, moss `#8EB092`. Warm, never black.

## Type
IBM Plex Sans for interface and documents in the default theme; IBM Plex Mono for raw Markdown. Other themes bring their own faces.

## Voice
Plain, direct, a little dry. Verbs over nouns. "Saved." not "Your changes have been saved successfully!" Errors say what happened and what to do next. Never "Oops", never "Awesome", never "supercharge".
